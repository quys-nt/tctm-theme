Company Introduction

TCTM is a privately-owned company headquartered in Vietnam. Our mission is to provide Vietnamese consumers with top-tier home appliances to enhance their quality of life. TCTM is the exclusive official distributor in Vietnam for products from the following brands: KDK (Japan), Kadeka (Japan), and Bertazzoni (Italy).

The company’s core values include:

  • Focusing on Customer: Delivering excellent service and support to meet the diverse needs of Vietnamese consumers.
  • Quality: Providing products with the highest standards of performance and reliability.
  • Integrity: Conducting business with honesty and transparency, placing customer experience satisfaction at the center.
  • Partnership: Building strong and long-term relationships with suppliers and distributors based on mutual trust and respect.
